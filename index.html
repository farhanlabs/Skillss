About Us

Hiring Built on Trust and Quality

Veritus Staffforce Private Limited is a staffing and recruitment company focused on one thing: putting the right people in the right roles. The name comes from veritas, the Latin word for truth, and it guides how we work.

Whether you need a single specialist or a full team, we handle sourcing, screening and coordination so you can focus on running your business.
